## Outline

Every scene note carries a small ` ```gtd-scene``` ` block (New scene adds it automatically; **Insert scene card** retrofits old notes). It renders a live card: click a status segment to move the pipeline, read the resolved next action and where it came from, toggle the flag, watch word progress. When your word count says you've outgrown the status — words on an outlined scene, goal reached while drafting — the card *suggests* the move; nothing ever changes without a click.
