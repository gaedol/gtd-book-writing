## Outline

The Available Now list answers "what can I actually sit down and work on?" A scene qualifies when: it isn't final, it has a next action, its defer date (if any) has passed, and — in a sequential chapter — every earlier scene is final. Everything else is hidden, on purpose. Explain defer as "not before", flag (★) as "today's focus", and why hiding blocked work removes the where-do-I-start paralysis.
