## Outline

**Compile manuscript** stitches your scenes — in manuscript order — into one note in `Notes/`. Choose which statuses to include (default: everything past `idea`), whether chapters get headings, and the scene separator. Frontmatter, the scene card block, and Beats are stripped; if a scene has a `## Draft` section, only that prose is taken. This manual's published copy is produced exactly this way.
