## Draft

The ledger was where she expected it, in the iron box under the counting-room floor, and the letter was folded inside the back cover where her father had kept the things he meant to send and never sent.

It was addressed to a name she did not know. Viktor Halsen. The hand was her father's, but smaller, the way a man writes when he does not want the writing to carry.

*If you are reading this,* it began, *then I am past the reach of the thing we did, and you are not.*
