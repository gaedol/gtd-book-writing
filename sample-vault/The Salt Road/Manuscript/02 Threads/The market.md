## Outline

Market day, but half the stalls are empty. The salt is still good; the buyers are gone. A stranger has been seen measuring the cliff road with a chain — the railway, everyone says, will save them or finish them, and no one can agree which. Mara, who knows everyone's books, quietly takes on a debt that isn't hers, for reasons we won't understand until Reckoning.
