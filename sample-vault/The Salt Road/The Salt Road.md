# The Salt Road

A novel. Anna Vereza returns to the salt town that bears her family's name to settle her dead father's debts — and finds the trade, and the family, built on a lie she helped tell.

## Premise

In the last decade of the salt trade, before the railways made the coastal pans worthless, a daughter comes home to a fortune that does not exist and a confession her father never made.

## Themes

- Inheritance as debt, not gift
- What a town agrees not to know about itself
