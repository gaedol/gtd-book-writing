# Research: the 1890s coastal salt trade

Background notes — keep the period plausible, not exhaustive.

- Solar evaporation in shallow pans; harvest in late summer.
- Decline driven by rail-carried mined salt undercutting coastal sea salt on price.
- A small pan operation ran on credit between harvests — debts rolled forward, settled (or not) at harvest. This is the financial world the Vereza debts live in.
- Watch anachronisms: no telephones in the town yet; letters and the weekly boat carry news.
