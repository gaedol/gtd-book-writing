# Synopsis

Anna Vereza returns to the failing salt town her family named, expecting to settle her dead father's debts and leave. In his ledger she finds an unsent letter to a stranger, Viktor Halsen, hinting that the family fortune — and the "& Son" the town never questioned — rests on something she and her father once did together. As Viktor rides in and the town's own troubles (a railway that will save or finish them) close around her, Anna must decide whether to let the lie surface and what, if anything, of the name is worth keeping.

## One line

A daughter comes home to a fortune that doesn't exist and a confession her father never made.
