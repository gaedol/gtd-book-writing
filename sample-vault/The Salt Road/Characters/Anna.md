# Anna Vereza

Protagonist. Late thirties. Left the salt town at twenty-six after a quarrel she has never explained; returns to settle her father's estate.

- **Wants:** to pay the debts and leave clean.
- **Needs:** to stop carrying the family lie as if it were hers alone.
- **Voice:** dry, precise, allergic to sentiment.

## Relationships
- [[Mara]] — knew her as a child; sees through her.
- [[Viktor]] — the name in the letter; unknown to her at the start.
